## Shared conventions

- **Sizes** are always `sm` (32px), `md` (40px, default), and `lg` (48px) for controls.
- **Focus** is always a 2px `color.border.focus` outline with a 2px offset, or the `shadow.focus` halo on text fields.
- **Disabled** controls use `color.bg.muted` and `color.text.disabled`. Prefer explaining why an action is unavailable over disabling it silently.
- **Polymorphism:** components that render a single element accept `asChild` to merge their styles onto your own element.
