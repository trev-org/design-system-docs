Elevation communicates what sits above what. Lumen uses five shadow levels, each with two layers: a tight contact shadow and a soft ambient one.

<Shadows />

## Elevation in dark mode

Shadows are barely visible on dark surfaces. In dark mode, Lumen components that use `shadow.sm` or higher also add a `color.border.subtle` border, and elevated surfaces step up from `neutral.950` (canvas) to `neutral.900` (surface).

## Guidelines

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Check>
