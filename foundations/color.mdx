Color in Lumen has two layers. **Primitives** are the raw palette. **Semantic tokens** give those colors a job, such as "surface" or "danger text", and switch values between light and dark mode.

<Note>
  Every value on this page is generated from `figma/tokens/primitives.json` and `figma/tokens/semantic/*.json` by `scripts/build-tokens.mjs`. Click any swatch to copy its value.
</Note>

## Primitive palette

**Iris** (`brand`) is Lumen's signature hue: a blue-violet that reads as confident without shouting. **Graphite** (`neutral`) is cool-tinted to sit comfortably next to it. Green, amber, red, and blue carry feedback meaning only.

## Usage

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Check>
    **Do** use semantic tokens in components: `var(--lumen-color-bg-brand)`, not `var(--lumen-color-brand-600)`.
  </Check>
  <Warning>
    **Don't** use feedback colors decoratively. Red always means danger, green always means success.
  </Warning>
  <Check>
    **Do** keep brand color for the single most important action and for selection states.
  </Check>
  <Warning>
    **Don't** convey meaning with color alone. Pair it with an icon, text, or pattern.
  </Warning>
</Columns>

### Color roles at a glance

| Role | Light | Dark | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canvas and surface | `base.white` | `neutral.950` / `neutral.900` | Page and card backgrounds |
| Primary text | `neutral.900` | `neutral.50` | Headings, body copy |
| Brand | `brand.600` | `brand.500` | Primary buttons, links, selection |
| Danger | `red.600` | `red.600` | Destructive buttons, errors |
| Focus | `brand.500` | `brand.400` | Focus rings on every interactive element |

See [Accessibility](/foundations/accessibility) for the contrast ratio of every key pairing.
